## Known limitations

- Regex extraction misses spelled-out numbers and vague time phrases ("twenty percent", "a couple of weeks").
- Relative-time phrases are treated as deadlines even when benign (e.g. "this month"), which can over-block.
- Unsupported claims without a number ("your favourite feature") need the semantic check, which is not implemented.
- The red-team set is written by the guardrail author (M3); expect optimistic recall.
