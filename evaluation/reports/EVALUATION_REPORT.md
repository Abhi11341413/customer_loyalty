## 2. Policy engine (M2)

**What is measured (CLAUDE.md §14.2).** Whether the deterministic billing, offer, contact and
strategy rules give the expected decision. Expected outcomes are hand-written case tables in
`evaluation/policy/cases/` (32 billing, 34 offer, 15 contact cases), derived from §6–7 and the
policy YAMLs — not copied from engine output. On top of that: the ten demo customers (§16) and
the hand-labelled scenarios (`evaluation/datasets/labelled_scenarios.jsonl`, M2 half: 23 cases,
ids C0101–C0123).

Reproduce: `python evaluation/policy/run.py` → `policy_evaluation.md` + `policy_metrics.json`.

The M2 labelled half also scores 23/23 exact segment sets (macro-F1 1.000, 0 severe errors) in
M1's classifier evaluation (`classification_labelled.md`).

**Findings during development.**
- *Wrong bill chosen (fixed).* The first billing engine followed §6.3's "latest unsettled
  bill". For C0006 that picked a November bill not yet due and missed the October bill that
  was 26 days overdue. The engine now reuses M1's bill selection (most severe recent bill), so
  classification and policy share one rule; regression case B31 and the C0006 test cover it.
- *Labelling slip (fixed in the labels, not the engine).* Twelve labelled scenarios first had
  bill state PAID although each has a November bill issued and not yet due; by §6.2 that is
  NOT_YET_DUE.

**Limitations.**
- Correctness is measured against our *assumed* policy (`docs/assumptions.md`, B/O/K/T rows),
  not a real company's policy. 100% means the code does what the YAML says, not that the
  YAML is right.
- Case tables and the M2 labelled half were written by the policy author; a second team
  member should spot-check them (§14.5 bias note).
- The contact gate's "same objective within 7 days" rule needs the chosen objective; in the
  orchestrated pipeline (contact checked per category first) it is covered by the 14-day
  marketing gap and the 7-day per-bill reminder gap instead.
- Offer selection among several eligible offers is by offer id; there is no ranking.
